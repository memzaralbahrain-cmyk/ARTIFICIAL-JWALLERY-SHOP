Privacy Policy

Al Memzar Star Jewellery W.L.L — Last updated: August 2026

Al Memzar Star Jewellery ("we," "our," "us") operates this app and website to showcase our fashion and silver jewellery catalog and to help customers place orders with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

This app itself does not require you to create an account, and we do not collect or store any personal information within the app.

  • Browsing the catalog: You can view our products, prices, and categories without providing any personal information.
  • Placing an order: When you tap "Order on WhatsApp," you are redirected to WhatsApp with a pre-filled message. Any personal information you choose to share at that point (such as your name, phone number, or delivery address) is sent directly to our WhatsApp Business number and is subject to WhatsApp's own Privacy Policy, not ours.
  • Leaving a review: If you choose to write a product review, you are similarly redirected to WhatsApp, where you may voluntarily share your name and feedback with us. Reviews you send may be displayed publicly on our catalog page at our discretion.
  • Currency and language preference: If you select a display currency, this choice is saved only on your own device (using your browser's local storage) so the app remembers your preference next time. This information is never transmitted to us or any third party.
  • General location (approximate): To suggest a default display currency, the app may check your approximate country using your IP address via a third-party service. This is used only to pre-select a currency and is not stored or linked to you personally.

2. How We Use Information

Any information you send us via WhatsApp (such as your name, phone number, and delivery address) is used solely to:

  • Process and confirm your order
  • Arrange delivery or pickup
  • Respond to product reviews or customer inquiries

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

This app links out to or relies on the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • WhatsApp — for order placement, customer support, and reviews
  • ipapi.co — to approximate your country for currency suggestions
  • open.er-api.com — to fetch public currency exchange rates

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party services.

4. Returns & Exchange Policy

Items purchased through Al Memzar Star Jewellery can be returned or exchanged within 3 days of delivery, provided the product is unused and in its original condition. To request a return or exchange, please contact us via WhatsApp.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data Security

Since this app does not collect or store personal data on our servers, there is no personal database at risk. Any information shared via WhatsApp is protected by WhatsApp's end-to-end encryption and security practices.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
