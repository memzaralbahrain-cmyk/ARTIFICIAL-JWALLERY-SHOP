Privacy Policy

Al Memzar Star Jewellery W.L.L · Last updated: 23 September 2026

Who we are

Al Memzar Star Jewellery W.L.L ("we", "us") is a jewellery business based at 329, Block 302, Building 447, Manama, Kingdom of Bahrain (CR / License No: 137064-1). This policy explains how the Memzar Mart app and website handle your information.

Information we do NOT collect

  • No account, sign-up or login is needed.
  • We do not collect your name, email, address, payment details or precise location.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we never sell your data.

Information stored on your device

To make the app work, the following is saved only on your own phone and is never uploaded to us:

  • Your cart and wishlist
  • Your order history (a list of orders you sent through WhatsApp)
  • Your language, currency and notification preferences
  • Cached product photos so the catalogue can work offline

You can delete order history from Account → Clear order history, or remove everything by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app.

Ordering through WhatsApp

Payments and orders are not processed inside the app. When you tap "Order on WhatsApp" or send a question, the app opens WhatsApp with a pre-written message. If you send it, we receive your WhatsApp number and the message you choose to send, which we use only to answer your question and fulfil your order. WhatsApp handles that data under its own privacy policy.

Notifications

Notifications are optional and off until you enable them. If you enable them, a device notification token may be stored so we can send you news about new collections and offers. You can turn them off anytime in your phone settings.

Hosting

The website files and photos are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, it may process technical information such as your IP address when the app loads content. We do not have access to a profile of you from this.

Children

The app is a general shopping catalogue and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

Because we hold no personal profile about you, there is nothing to log in to or export. If you have sent us a WhatsApp message and would like it deleted, message us and we will delete it from our side.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
